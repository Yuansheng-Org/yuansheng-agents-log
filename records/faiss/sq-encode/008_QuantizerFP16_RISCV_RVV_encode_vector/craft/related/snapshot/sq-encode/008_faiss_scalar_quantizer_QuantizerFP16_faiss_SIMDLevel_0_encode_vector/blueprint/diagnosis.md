  Reasoning: Disassembly of the target (35e73ea6) shows the whole loop 0x3895c6-0x38960e is the inlined encode\_fp16 with constants hoisted outside the loop (t1/t4/t5/t3/a6/fa3/fa4) but data-dependent scalar special-value handling per element; hotspot packet (e9d2d827) attributes 50%+50% to the lui a5,0x8 and addw a5,a5,t5 lines in that body. - `probable` (contributing factor, medium) Within the scalar loop the single most-sampled instruction (0x3895c8 lui a5,0x8, 50% of in-function samples) is the first half of per-iteration rematerialization of the loop-invariant fp16 NaN constant 0x7E00 (lui a5,0x8; addi a5,a5,-512). The compiler hoisted all other loop-invariant constants (0x7fffffff, 0x7f800000, 0x8000, 0x1000, 0x7ffff000, float magic, clamp max) out of the loop but reuses a5 for the conversion result, forcing 2 extra instructions per ~18-instruction iteration.
  Reasoning: The annotate (35e73ea6) shows constants t1/a6/t4/t5/t3/fa3/fa4 initialized once at 0x3895ae-0x3895c4 while a5=0x7E00 is rebuilt at 0x3895c8-0x3895ca on every pass; the loop merge at 0x3895fc consumes a5 from three definitions (regular conversion, NaN, Inf) so the compiler chose unconditional re-materialization. hotspot packet (e9d2d827) ranks 0x3895c8 as top sample. This is a compiler code-generation shape (register allocation merges the invariant with the scratch register), not a semantic necessity.

## Actions
